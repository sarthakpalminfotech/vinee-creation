## 0.1 Safety: nothing that works today may break

These rules come **before everything else** in this document. If any instruction later conflicts with them, these win.

**C. Change carefully**
7. **One fix at a time**, in its own commit, touching only the files that fix needs. No unrelated reformatting, renaming, file moves, or "cleanup while I'm here".
8. **No dependency upgrades, no new frameworks, no config/build changes** unless a fix strictly needs it. If it does, explain why and ask me first.
9. Don't change shared code (common components, utilities, API helpers, the database layer) without first **finding every place that uses it** and re-checking those places after the change.

**D. Check after every fix**
11. After each fix:
    - run the type check / build / linter / tests
    - re-check the flow you fixed
    - **re-run every item on the regression checklist that could be affected**

    After each group of fixes, re-run the **whole** checklist.
12. If something that worked before is now broken, **undo that fix** (`git revert` that commit), note it in `docs/AUDIT.md`, and find a safer way. Never leave the project in a broken state between steps.
13. At the end, the full regression checklist and the end-to-end scenario (§4) must both pass. List anything you couldn't verify, and why.

**E. When unsure, ask**
14. If you aren't sure whether a change could affect working behaviour or real data, **stop and ask me** before making it.

## 2. The business process (source of truth)

Vinee Creation is a **trader**: it doesn't manufacture. It orders grey (undyed) fabric from **weavers**, gets it dyed/finished at **mills**, folds it, and sells finished fabric to **customers**.

### 2.1 Masters
- **R-M-1 Party groups and parties.**
  - One owner can run several firms. For example, the **party group "Ganesh"** contains the parties **Ganesh Textiles**, **Ganesh Fabrics** and **Ganesh Fashion**.
  - A party group has many parties; a party belongs to one group.
  - **POs are placed with a party group**; **goods are received from a specific party** of that group.
- **R-M-2 Quality: two names.**
  - Every quality has an **original name** (the one the weaver uses, e.g. "Nylon") and a **nickname** (the name used with mills and customers, e.g. "Gold Lava").
  - **Documents to weavers** use the original name.
  - **Documents to mills and customers** (mill challans, programs, customer-facing screens) **always use the nickname**.
  - Every grey PO and challan has a quality.
- **R-M-3 Mills:** the mills fabric is sent to for dyeing (and that weavers can deliver to directly).
- **R-M-4 Physical locations:** **shop → rack**, e.g. **Shop 6065, Rack-A**. Every taka in our godown has exactly one current location.
- **R-M-5 Shades:** a **shade chart** (e.g. "Chart A") with **shade numbers** (e.g. 36, 37, 39), each with its **colour** (a colour value and/or swatch image) so it can be printed on the program PDF.
- **R-M-6 Customers** (for sales orders / availability).

### 2.2 Grey purchase order (PO)
- **R-PO-1** A PO is created for a **party group**, with: quality, **ordered meters** (e.g. 50,000 m), **rate per meter** (e.g. ₹3.50), and the **PO number from the party's paper PO**, entered by the user. Date and notes are optional.
- **R-PO-2** The PO list shows ordered meters, received meters, pending meters and a status: **Open / Partly received / Received / Closed (short)**.
- **R-PO-3** A PO can be received **in full or in parts**, over several challans, from **any party in its group**.
- **R-PO-4 Weaver shrinkage:** the weaver delivers takas whose total can be **less than ordered** (e.g. 49,300 m for a 50,000 m PO). The PO must show **ordered vs received** and the **weaver shrinkage** (meters and %) when it's closed / fully received.

### 2.3 Receiving grey fabric (weaver challan)
- **R-GR-1** A grey receipt is entered against a PO with:
  - the **party** (one of the PO's group parties)
  - the weaver's **challan number** and date
  - the **destination**: **our godown** or **directly to a mill** (which mill)
- **R-GR-2** A challan has **many takas** (e.g. a 50,000 m PO can be ~500 takas). Each taka has a **taka number** and **meters with 2 decimals**: typically 96–104 m, but values like 94.69, 102.58, 103.5 must be accepted exactly.
  - Entry must be **fast for hundreds of rows**: a keyboard-friendly grid, add rows quickly, paste from Excel if possible, running totals (taka count, meters) visible all the time.
  - Block duplicate taka numbers within the challan, and decide (open question if unclear) whether they must be unique system-wide.
- **R-GR-3** Receiving updates the PO's received meters and status.
- **R-GR-4 Direct-to-mill receipts** put the takas straight into **grey at mill** inventory (for that mill), **without** our own mill challan. They never pass through our godown or a rack.

### 2.4 Rack assignment
- **R-LOC-1** After receiving into our godown, the user **selects many takas at once** (select all / range / individually) and **assigns them to one rack** in one action. Example: 24 takas → Shop 6065, Rack-A.
- **R-LOC-2** Takas can be **moved** to another rack later, and the location history is kept.
- **R-LOC-3** Grey stock at our place can be viewed **by location** (shop → rack → takas, with counts and meters).

### 2.5 Sending grey to the mill (our mill challan)
- **R-SM-1** The user picks takas to send, usually by selecting a **received weaver challan**, and **may send only some of them** (e.g. 36 of 48).
- **R-SM-2 Picking list:** the screen and the printout show the supervisor **where to take each selected taka from**, grouped by shop → rack (taka numbers, count and meters per rack).
- **R-SM-3** The mill challan shows the **mill**, the **quality nickname** (never the original name), **taka list, total takas and total meters**, and can be **printed / downloaded**.
- **R-SM-4** On sending, those takas leave our godown/rack inventory and become **grey at mill** for that mill. Takas not sent stay where they are.
- **R-SM-5** A taka can't be sent twice or from the wrong state. All of this happens in one transaction.

### 2.6 Mill program (dyeing instructions)
- **R-PG-1** A **program** is given to a mill **in takas**, e.g. "48 takas of Gold Lava, shade chart A".
- **R-PG-2** The takas of a program can be **divided across shade numbers**, e.g. 24 takas shade 37 + 24 takas shade 39. The split must add up to the program's takas.
- **R-PG-3** The **program PDF** shows: program number, mill, quality **nickname**, shade chart, and each shade number **with its colour printed**, plus takas and meters per shade and totals.
- **R-PG-4** Program takas can only come from **grey at that mill** (sent by us or delivered directly by the weaver).

### 2.7 Receiving finished fabric from the mill
- **R-FR-1** The mill sends a challan **referring to the program/challan number**. For each taka it has the **grey meter** and the **finished meter**.
- **R-FR-2 Finish shrinkage** per taka = grey meter − finished meter (and %). Totals per challan / program.
- **R-FR-3** Partial returns are possible. The program shows received vs pending takas per shade.
- **R-FR-4** Received takas become **finished goods**, with quality + shade. Takas still at the mill under a program are **in dyeing**.

### 2.8 Folding
- **R-FD-1** Staff fold the finished takas and enter the **actual folded meters** per taka, because the mill's finished meters can be wrong.
- **R-FD-2 Folding shrinkage** per taka = finished meter − folded meter.
- **R-FD-3** After folding, the folded meters are the meters used for stock and sales.

### 2.9 Wastage / shrinkage report
- **R-SH-1** For **each taka**, show the full chain:
  - grey meters (weaver challan)
  - → finished meters (mill)
  - → folded meters

  with the loss at each step and the **total loss from start to end** (meters and %).
- **R-SH-2** For takas that went **directly from the weaver to the mill**, the weaver part is left out (start from the mill's grey meter).
- **R-SH-3** Summaries by PO, weaver/party, quality, mill and program. **Weaver shrinkage** is also shown at the PO level (ordered vs received, §2.2).

### 2.10 Inventory views
There are four kinds of stock. Each can be **filtered by quality and by weaver (party / group)**, and also by mill, shade and location where it applies:

| View | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Grey at our place** | received into our godown, not yet sent; by shop/rack |
| **Grey at mill** | sent by us + delivered directly by the weaver, not yet in a program |
| **In dyeing** | under a program at a mill, not yet received back; by shade |
| **Finished goods** | received from the mill (and folded), by quality + shade |

Show taka counts and meters everywhere. Every meter total must reconcile with the taka rows.

### 2.11 Customer order and availability
- **R-SO-1** When a customer orders, e.g. **"Gold Lava, 500 takas lump: 50 takas shade 38, 50 takas shade 39, …"**, the user must immediately see, **for that quality (nickname) and each requested shade**:
  - finished goods available (takas, meters)
  - in dyeing at mills with that shade (takas, meters, which program/mill)
  - grey at mill not yet programmed
  - grey at our place (by location)
- **R-SO-2** If customer orders already exist in the code, check they work with this. If they don't exist, build the **availability view** first. Raise order booking / reservation as an open question rather than inventing allocation rules.

### 2.12 General quality bar
- **R-G-1** The UI must be **easy and appealing**: clean, consistent, responsive on phone, tablet and desktop, with no broken layouts.
- **R-G-2** Every list has search, sensible filters, pagination or virtualised scrolling for large data, and loading / empty / error states.
- **R-G-3** Every form has validation with clear inline messages. Saving can't double-submit.
- **R-G-4** Meters are stored with exact decimal precision, never as floating point that drifts. Totals are rounded to 2 decimals only for display.
- **R-G-5** Every multi-row change (receipt, rack assignment, send to mill, program, mill receipt, folding) is **one transaction**, with state checks so a taka can't be in two places or two states.
- **R-G-6** Each taka has a clear **state machine**:
  - received at godown → (racked) → sent to mill / direct at mill → in program (dyeing) → finished → folded
  - plus a **history of every movement**

  Invalid transitions are rejected by the server, not only hidden in the UI.

## 8. Known issues reported by the team
